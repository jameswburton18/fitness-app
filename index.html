Why this plan

Right knee

Pain in deep bends and when you tense the quad, crackling, a slow onset, and no swelling or giving way: that fits patellofemoral pain, irritation where the kneecap glides on the thigh bone. It’s the most common knee pain in active adults. It’s a load problem, not wear and tear.

The best evidence (JOSPT 2019 guideline; BJSM 2024 best-practice guide) is hip and knee strengthening, about 3 times a week, for 6–12 weeks. Higher-volume programs did better. Hence three rehab days, a knee ladder ordered from least to most kneecap load, and a side-of-hip ladder for the inward knee drift.

The crackling is common in pain-free knees too (about a third have it). It isn’t a sign of damage and isn’t a reason to avoid loading.

Big toe

The kick injured the big toe’s main joint and the tissue under it. It moves fine unloaded. What’s missing is strength when it’s bent back under load, which is sprint push-off or kneeling on tucked toes. The Big-toe push-off ladder rebuilds exactly that, a little more range and load each rung. The evidence here is thin (expert opinion, not trials), so the benchmarks are how we’ll know it’s working.

Back and hamstring

These are working, so they’re unchanged: the McGill Big 3 (curl-up, side plank, bird dog) and hinge patterning for the disc, and slider curls (the home Nordic curl) for the hamstring.

For the next 4 weeks
  • Avoid sitting on your haunches and deep squatting outside the app. Deep bends under load are what the kneecap likes least. Depth comes back as the knee ladder climbs.
  • Don’t kneel on tucked toes outside the app. The toe ladder brings that position back gradually.
  • Stairs and your 10k steps are fine. Keep them.
  • Judge progress by the fortnightly benchmarks, not day to day. Kneecap pain usually takes 6–12 weeks to settle.
Pain traffic light

Use this during any exercise, any day:

0–3 out of 10, settled by the next morning: carry on, and level up when it’s easy. Mild discomfort during rehab loading is normal.

4–5, or not settled by the next morning: stay on that rung or drop one, or halve the range. Don’t skip it, shrink it.

6+, sharp, or pain that changes how you move: stop that exercise today and come back a rung lower next session.

When to level up

Finish all sets at the top of the range, feeling like you had 2–3 more reps in the tank and within the green light, two sessions in a row. Then take the next rung. The app asks after each workout. Be honest in both directions.

Back to running and football

Running puts about 4–5× bodyweight through the kneecap, and it’s the same toe push-off that re-hurt it. So it’s gated:

  1. 25 single-leg calf raises each side, toe kneel ≤2/10 and step-downs ≤2/10 → start pogo hops (toe ladder).
  2. 3 × 20s pogo hops, fine the next morning → walk-jog: 1 min jog / 1 min walk × 8, three times a week. Add a minute of jogging per block each week while it stays green.
  3. 20 min easy continuous jog plus comfortable skipping → strides at 70–80%, then 90%.
  4. Strides feel normal → turning and cutting drills, then a training session before a match. Stiff-soled boots or a carbon insole protect the toe.

There’s no validated return-to-sport test for kneecap pain. These are sensible checkpoints, not guarantees.
